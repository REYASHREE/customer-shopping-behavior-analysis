# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

### Turning Customer Purchase Data into Actionable Business Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

Understanding why customers buy, what influences their purchasing
decisions, and which factors drive revenue is essential for
retail businesses to remain competitive.

This project explores customer shopping behavior to uncover
purchasing trends, evaluate customer preferences, and identify
opportunities to improve sales performance and customer retention.

Using data analytics techniques, the project aims to translate
raw customer transaction data into meaningful business insights
that can support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing patterns and spending behavior.
- Identify high-performing product categories and customer segments.
- Investigate the relationship between discounts and purchasing decisions.
- Examine how subscription status relates to customer spending.
- Evaluate customer ratings and purchasing preferences.
- Develop actionable recommendations to improve retail performance.

## 🔍 Key Business Questions

### Customer Behavior
- Which customer segments contribute most to sales?
- How does customer purchasing behavior vary across age groups?
- Are repeat customers exhibiting different purchasing patterns?

### Product & Revenue Analysis
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which products receive the strongest customer ratings?
- How do purchase amounts vary across product categories?

### Discounts & Subscriptions
- How does the use of discounts relate to purchase amounts?
- Do subscribed customers exhibit different spending patterns?
- Which customer groups could be targeted for retention campaigns?

## 📊 Analytical Approach

1. **Data Understanding:** Examine the dataset structure, variables,
   and business context.

2. **Data Preparation:** Identify missing values, duplicates,
   inconsistencies, and data quality issues.

3. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Investigate customer demographics,
   purchasing behavior, product preferences, and spending patterns.

4. **SQL Analysis:** Use filtering, aggregation, joins, and
   analytical queries to answer relevant business questions.

5. **Data Visualization:** Present key metrics and patterns through
   clear visualizations and an interactive dashboard, where applicable.

6. **Business Recommendations:** Translate analytical findings into
   practical recommendations for retail decision-makers.

## 💡 Expected Business Value

The analysis aims to help retail businesses:

- Understand customer segments and their purchasing preferences.
- Identify opportunities to improve product and sales strategies.
- Evaluate customer engagement and retention opportunities.
- Make more informed, data-driven business decisions.
